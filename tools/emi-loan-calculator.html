Take Control of Your Debt

When a bank offers you a loan, the interest rate can be deceiving. A 7% loan over 5 years costs vastly more than a 7% loan over 3 years. This EMI calculator allows you to input the principal amount, interest rate, and tenure to instantly reveal your exact monthly payment and the total amount of interest the bank is extracting from you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI stand for?

Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount you pay the bank every month, which includes both a portion of the principal and the interest.

Why does the bank take so much interest upfront?

Standard loans use an amortization schedule. Because your principal balance is highest at the beginning, the interest generated on that balance is also highest in the first few years.
