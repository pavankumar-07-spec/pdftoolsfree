Free Online Assignment & Grade Tracker - Organize Homework & Deadlines

Juggling multiple university courses, lab reports, essays, midterms, and project deadlines across separate syllabi can quickly lead to stress and missed due dates. Our Free Online Assignment & Grade Tracker helps high school, college, and university students organize their semester workload in one centralized, auto-sorting dashboard. Track assignment statuses, calculate grade weightages, monitor upcoming due dates, and boost your academic productivity.

How to Use the Assignment & Grade Tracker (Step-by-Step Guide)

Organizing your academic tasks and semester goals is quick and straightforward. Follow these 4 easy steps:

1.

Input Course & Assignment Details: Add assignment titles, course codes (e.g., CS101, MATH201), category types (Homework, Quiz, Essay, Exam), and target due dates.

2.

Set Grades & Weightages: Enter maximum points or percentage weights for each task to track your cumulative course scores accurately.

3.

Review Automated Chronological Sorting: The tracker automatically organizes tasks by due date, highlighting pending assignments due within 48 hours in urgent alert colors.

4.

Mark Complete & Track Progress: Check off completed tasks as you submit them to move them to your completed archive and update your overall semester progress percentage.

Key Features of Our Assignment & Homework Tracker

  • Smart Chronological Auto-Sorting: Your workload is mathematically sorted by urgency so you always know what task requires your immediate focus.
  • Visual Color-Coded Deadline Alerts: High-priority items due within 24 to 48 hours are automatically highlighted in prominent warning colors.
  • Grade & Course Performance Monitoring: Keep track of scores across assignments, tests, and projects to estimate final subject grades and maintain your GPA targets.
  • 100% Offline & Private Storage: All course lists, notes, and grade records are saved locally in your browser's secure LocalStorage. No server accounts or logins required.
  • Consolidates Multiple Syllabi: Replace tedious manual planners or scattered portal dashboards with a single unified homework tracker.
  • Mobile & Responsive Layout: Seamlessly manage your academic schedule on mobile smartphones, tablets, or laptops between lectures.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this Assignment Tracker free for students?

Yes! Our assignment and grade tracker is 100% free with unlimited tasks, courses, and grade entries for students worldwide.

Is my academic data stored on an external server?

No. All data is saved exclusively inside your web browser's local cache (LocalStorage). Your assignment names, dates, and grades remain private on your device.

Will my assignment data be saved if I refresh or close my browser?

Yes! Because data is stored in your browser's persistent LocalStorage, your assignment list will remain intact whenever you return to the page on the same browser.

Can I track assignments for multiple subjects or semesters?

Yes! You can organize assignments across unlimited subjects, classes, and course categories within the tracker UI.
