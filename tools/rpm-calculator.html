Motor and Machining RPM Formulas

Synchronous speed ($N_s$) is the rotation frequency of the magnetic field in an AC motor. Induction motors run slightly slower due to slip.

Formulas

$$N_s = \frac{120 \times f}{P} \quad (\text{Motor Sync Speed RPM})$$
$$N = N_s \times (1 - \text{Slip}) \quad (\text{Full Load Rotor RPM})$$
$$N = \frac{1000 \times V_c}{\pi \times D} \quad (\text{CNC Machining RPM})$$
