What is Capacitive Reactance?

Capacitive Reactance ($X_C$) is the opposition offered by a capacitor to the flow of Alternating Current (AC). Unlike pure resistance, capacitive reactance decreases as frequency increases.

Formula

$$X_C = \frac{1}{2 \pi f C}$$
