Quadratic Equation Solver

Solve quadratic equations instantly. Our free Quadratic Solver calculates both real and complex roots locally in your browser, ensuring your math homework remains totally private.

What is the Quadratic Equation Solver?

The quadratic formula `(-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a` is a staple of high school algebra and advanced physics. Manually calculating the roots—especially when dealing with negative discriminants that result in imaginary numbers—is tedious and error-prone. This solver provides an instant, mathematically perfect solution. You simply input your 'a', 'b', and 'c' coefficients, and the tool evaluates the discriminant to output the exact roots. Built as a secure, client-side utility, it performs all arithmetic in your browser, ensuring your academic interactions are never tracked.

Why Use Our Quadratic Equation Solver?

  • Handles Imaginary Numbers: Flawlessly calculates complex roots if the discriminant is negative.
  • Instant Evaluation: Solves the equation the moment you click calculate.
  • 100% Private: We do not log or save your math equations.
  • Educational Tool: Helps verify your manual homework calculations.

How to Use the Quadratic Equation Solver

1.

Ensure your equation is in standard format: ax² + bx + c = 0.

2.

Enter the numerical values for the a, b, and c coefficients.

3.

Click 'Solve Equation'.

4.

Review the calculated roots (X1 and X2) and the discriminant value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if 'a' is zero?

If 'a' is zero, it is not a quadratic equation (it becomes a linear equation). The tool will prompt you to enter a valid 'a' coefficient.

Does it show the step-by-step working?

Currently, it provides the evaluated discriminant and the final roots. Step-by-step breakdown is planned for a future update.

Is my data sent to a math API?

No, all mathematics are executed natively by your browser's JavaScript engine.
