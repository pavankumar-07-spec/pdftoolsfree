LMTD Formula

LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2)

In counter-flow heat exchangers, $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,in} - T_{c,out}$ and $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,out} - T_{c,in}$.
