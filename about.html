Our Mission

"Every student's semester companion."

We believe every student and professional deserves access to powerful academic and utility tools — without paying a subscription, without creating an account, and without being tracked. FreeToolsPDF is our answer to that belief, providing a platform where anyone can manage their daily tasks securely and efficiently.

📚

Our Story

It started with a simple frustration: why are there no good, free, India-focused tools for college students? Attendance calculators with wrong formulas. CGPA calculators that require login. PDF mergers with watermarks.

We built FreeToolsPDF to change that. Every tool is designed specifically for the Indian university system — 75% attendance rules, 10-point CGPA scales, and semester patterns. Beyond students, we offer universally essential PDF, Image, Developer, File, and Text tools that process everything locally in your browser to ensure absolute privacy and security for your sensitive documents and code. No generic, one-size-fits-all solutions, just tools that work seamlessly.
